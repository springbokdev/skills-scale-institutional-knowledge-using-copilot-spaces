# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection describes how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and improves cross-functional projects. It provides practical guidance for project teams and stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

## Project management process

OctoAcme uses an iterative lifecycle, with clear ownership and regular communication:

1. **Initiate:** Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and agree whether the work should move into planning.
2. **Plan:** Break approved work into prioritized, estimable increments; define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done; and map milestones, risks, and dependencies.
3. **Execute and track:** Deliver in small increments using a project board, regular team check-ins, reviews, testing, and progress metrics. Surface blockers and escalate them through the agreed path.
4. **Release:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans; deploy and verify the release, then communicate it to stakeholders.
5. **Improve:** Hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents. Assign owners and due dates to a small set of improvement actions and follow up on their impact.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication. Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers and QA collaborate on implementation and quality; stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams adapt their meeting cadence to the project, while keeping status, risks, and decisions visible.
